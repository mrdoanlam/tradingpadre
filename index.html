GETTING STARTED

Four steps, no prior experience required.

This is the same order most first-time users go through — skipping steps is usually where people run into avoidable problems.

01

Install a wallet for the network you plan to use

Phantom or Solflare for Solana; MetaMask or Rabby for Ethereum, Base, or BNB Chain. Pick one — you don't need every network on day one.

02

Connect it to the terminal

This only shares your public address — never your seed phrase. Any prompt asking for your seed phrase at this stage is not legitimate.

03

Set a session password and enable 2FA

Encryption happens client-side — there's no "forgot password" recovery through support, so treat it like a wallet password.

04

Fund your wallet and place a small first trade

Keep a small gas buffer separate from your trade amount. A small test trade confirms everything's wired up before you size up.

WORTH KNOWING

A few things beginners usually ask about.

01

Self-custody means no recovery button

If you lose access to your wallet, there's no support ticket that gets it back — the trade-off for not needing anyone's permission to trade.

02

Slippage tolerance isn't a bug

Wider slippage settings exist specifically because early-stage tokens move fast — it's a deliberate trade-off, not a default you should ignore.

03

Liquidity depth matters more than price

A token can look cheap and still be a bad trade if there isn't enough liquidity to exit the position later.

04

Cashback lowers cost, not risk

The 35% fee rebate reduces what you pay to trade — it doesn't offset a losing position, and it's not a reason to trade more than planned.
