Questions before you start

Is this the official Padre Terminal site? +

No — this is an independent referral page that links to the official terminal through a referral code. See the disclosure in the footer.

Do I need to sign up here? +

No account or form — the link takes you directly to the terminal, where you connect a wallet instead of creating a login.

Is "Padre" the same as "Terminal"? +

Yes. The platform was renamed from Padre to Terminal after Pump.fun's acquisition in October 2025 — same engine and self-custody model, new name and ownership.

Does the 35% cashback cost anything extra? +

No — it's a rebate on the fee you'd already pay per trade, credited automatically in SOL.

What if I lose access to my wallet? +

Recovery depends entirely on your wallet provider's own backup method — the terminal never held your funds, so it has no separate recovery flow.

Can I trade on more than one network? +

Yes — Solana, Ethereum, Base, and BNB Chain are all available from the same login. Most people start with one and add others later.

Is "trade padre gg" the right site, or is it "trade.padre"? +

They're the same destination — the actual address is trade.padre.gg, sometimes typed as "trade padre gg" or "trade.padre" in search since the exact domain format isn't always obvious. Every "Open Padre Terminal" link on this page goes to that same trade.padre.gg address through our referral code.
